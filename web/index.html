SIH PS 26066

Subsurface ocean temperature from surface satellites

Two models map eight daily surface fields to potential temperature at 15 depths over the North Indian Ocean.

Region 5°N–30°N, 45°E–105°E · 0.25° · JFM 2015–2024 (903 days)

Problem

Satellites observe the surface. The task is to reconstruct θ from 0 m to 1000 m from SST, SSS, SLA, ADT, surface currents, and winds.

Inputs (X)
8 surface channels
Labels (Y)
θ at 15 depths
Split
80 / 20 by time
Metric
Ocean-pixel RMSE (°C)

Models

Compare the satellite-only Vision Transformer with the lag ConvLSTM (3-day memory).

Vision Transformer

Splits each day’s surface map into patches, encodes them with a small Transformer, and decodes all 15 depth channels. Uses surface fields only — the fair SIH baseline.

  • Overall val RMSE ≈ 0.64 °C
  • Thermocline (100–150 m) ≈ 1.1 °C
  • Notebook: train_vit.ipynb

ConvLSTM lag

Takes three days (t−2, t−1, t). Lag days include surface fields plus shallow θ (0–200 m). Useful when the model runs daily and can reuse yesterday’s state.

  • Teacher forcing (true lags) ≈ 0.24 °C
  • Closed-loop (own lags) ≈ 1.61 °C
  • Notebook: train_convlstm_lag.ipynb
